CheckIn More is a daily check-in app for safety and peace of mind, with scheduled check-ins and instant alerts to your loved ones.

The idea behind CheckIn More came from family who were looking for a more consistent and automated method of checking in on their elderly parents.
Something more practical than texting "Are you alive?" every day...

The idea seemed simple enough: push notifications to check-in, that if missed would text your contacts.

In reality: designing a mobile app for both Android & iOS with a backend that continually monitors for missed check-ins is no small feat.

After months of late nights & lots of coffee, the apps are finally live on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/checkin-more/id6748661948) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hydroxygenlabs.armedis) 🎉

## Lessons Learned

**Avoid mobile apps at all costs.** Dealing with the app stores is a huge pain, and it's something AI still can't do for you.

**Safety apps have real stakes.** When a personal safety app doesn't work, people get upset, and outages are actually impactful. So I've invested a lot into reliability and testing to prevent them. The tradeoff is that I move a lot slower with AI here, because I'm a lot more cautious.

## Tech Stack

Basically just a couple Python containers running on Azure.

- Mobile App
  - React Native
  - TanStack Query
  - Tamagui
- Cloud
  - Azure PostgreSQL
  - Azure Container Apps
  - Azure Key Vault
  - Azure App Configuration
  - Axiom (OpenTelemetry)
- Container
  - FastAPI
  - SQLModel
  - SQLAlchemy
  - AP Scheduler
- Services
  - Clerk (Auth)
  - Twilio (SMS)
  - ElevenLabs (Agentic Phone Calls)
  - RevenueCat (Subscriptions)
