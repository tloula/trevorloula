Cedar Mingle went viral in my tiny school and saw around 1/8 of the student body create accounts.

## Ancient History

I created Cedar Mingle over the course of ~7 weeks during the height of the rona lockdowns.
I launched a couple weeks into the fall semester of my Senior year of college.
I wasn't sure what to expect - and was shocked that it went viral and became the talk of the school.

While no longer maintained, it was an extremely educational and fulfilling experience.

## Round Two

I'm trying to bring Cedar Mingle back. Over the years, people kept bringing it up and saying they wished it was still around, and I never had the time to rebuild a free app on my own.

But AI is finally good enough to do like 99% of the work for me, so I figured it'd be fun to revive it. It's been rebuilt from scratch, this time built to keep running for every class that comes next.

I don't know if it'll work, or if there's still enough interest. But at least I'll find out in January.

The whole story is on the [about page](https://cedarmingle.com/about?utm_source=trevorloula&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=cedar-mingle&utm_content=project).
